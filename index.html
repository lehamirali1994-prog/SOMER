Article 1 — Who We Are

A system built on evidence, not assumptions

SOMER is a community-based organization operating in Kakuma Refugee Camp, integrating research, skills development, and livelihood opportunities into one unified system of support for marginalized and displacement-affected communities.

Mission

To connect research, skills, and opportunity systems in order to enable sustainable livelihoods for marginalized and displacement-affected communities.

Vision

A world where individuals in marginalized and emerging regions have equitable access to opportunities to learn, grow, and sustain their livelihoods.

Approach

Systems-based and community-driven — every intervention is informed by evidence, responsive to real needs, and grounded in local context.

Article 2 — Purpose

Bridging the gap between learning and livelihood

SOMER exists to close the space between education systems, skills acquisition, and access to sustainable economic opportunity — with particular attention to refugees, host communities, youth, and women.

01

Integrates research, skills development, and livelihood opportunities into one unified system of support, rather than treating them as separate programs.

02

Bridges gaps between education systems, skills acquisition pathways, and access to sustainable economic opportunities.

03

Promotes inclusive, equitable, and sustainable development, prioritizing marginalized populations.

04

Operates strictly on a non-profit basis — every resource is reinvested into its objectives and activities, never distributed to members.

Article 8 — Membership

Join the system

Membership is open regardless of nationality, refugee or displacement status, ethnicity, gender, religion, or disability.

Who can join

Eligibility

  • At least 18 years of age
  • Resides in Kenya — refugees, asylum seekers, and stateless persons with valid UNHCR or GoK documentation, and host community members
  • Supports SOMER's mission, vision, and values
  • Willing to abide by the Constitution
  • Application approved by the Management Committee within 14 days
Your rights

As a member

  • Participate in all programs, activities, and meetings
  • Vote in General Meetings and elections
  • Stand for election as an office bearer
  • Access organizational information relevant to membership
  • Raise concerns and be heard by the Management Committee
Your commitment

Responsibilities

  • Uphold the Constitution and decisions made at duly convened meetings
  • Attend meetings and participate actively
  • Respect fellow members and governance structures
  • Declare conflicts of interest where relevant
  • Sign the Member Commitment Declaration on admission

Ready to sign the commitment declaration?

Every member reads the Constitution, signs on admission, and joins the official register.

Articles 9–12 — Governance

How SOMER is run

A five-person Management Committee holds oversight and decision-making, elected for two-year terms and accountable to the full membership at every Annual General Meeting.

2 yrsTerm of office, renewable for one further term
MonthlyManagement Committee meetings
3 of 5Signatories on every financial transaction (2 of 3 required)
3/4Quorum required for a committee meeting
I

Chairperson

Presides over meetings, acts as official spokesperson, guides decision-making, and is a financial signatory.

II

Vice Chairperson

Supports the Chairperson and assumes their duties in their absence, excluding financial signatory authority.

III

Secretary

Maintains records, minutes, and the membership register; handles correspondence; a financial signatory.

IV

Vice Secretary

Supports the Secretary and stands in for records and communication duties in their absence.

V

Treasurer

Oversees all finances, budgets, and bank accounts; presents financial reports monthly; a financial signatory.

The Committee reflects diverse, inclusive representation across nationalities and genders. Decisions are made by simple majority, sensitive matters go to secret ballot, and any office bearer may be removed by a two-thirds vote at a General Meeting following a fair hearing.
